# Quick Pizza Dough
- **Prep Time**: 10 minutes
- **Rise Time**: 30-60 minutes [20-40 min at 5,280 ft] <!-- CHECK -->
- **Total Time**: 55-80 minutes (including baking) [45-60 min at 5,280 ft] <!-- CHECK -->
- **Yield**: 1 large pizza or 2 medium pizzas
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Type**: Dinner/Base Recipe
- **Source**: Personal method, originally developed at high altitude (5,280 ft)

### Ingredients
- [ ] **Warm water** (~240ml) - *Location: tap water*
- [ ] **Active dry or instant yeast** (~2 tbsp, generous amount for fast rise) - *Location: pantry/fridge*
- [ ] **Maple syrup** (dollop, ~1 tbsp) - *Location: pantry*
- [ ] **Semolina flour** (205g [1¼ cups]) - *Location: pantry* <!-- CHECK -->
- [ ] **Plain flour** (40g+, add by feel) - *Location: pantry*

### Equipment
- [ ] **Pizza stone** - *preheat in oven*
- [ ] **Baking paper** - *for easy transfer*
- [ ] **Pizza spatula/peel** - *for transferring to oven*

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Activate Yeast
1. Combine ~240ml warm water, ~2 tbsp yeast, and a dollop of maple syrup in a bowl
2. Stir and let activate briefly (yeast will start to foam)

#### Step 2: Preheat Oven
3. Place pizza stone in oven and preheat to **290°C / 270°C fan / gas 9** [550°F] <!-- CHECK -->
4. Let stone preheat for at least 30-45 minutes for best results

#### Step 3: Mix Dough
5. Add **205g semolina flour** to the yeast mixture and stir
6. Add **40g plain flour** to start
7. Continue adding plain flour gradually until dough is slightly sticky but workable

#### Step 4: Knead and Develop Gluten
8. Work the dough by hand, kneading until gluten begins to form
9. Dough should become smooth and elastic (slightly sticky is okay)
10. Knead for 3-5 minutes total

#### Step 5: Rise
11. Cover bowl with clean towel or cling film
12. Let rise in warm spot until doubled in size (30-60 minutes [20-40 min at 5,280 ft]) <!-- CHECK -->
13. Meanwhile, prep your toppings

#### Step 6: Shape and Bake
14. Shape dough into a round directly on a piece of baking paper
15. Add your toppings
16. Use pizza spatula to transfer pizza AND baking paper onto the preheated stone
17. **Turn oven down to 180°C / 160°C fan / gas 4** once pizza goes in
18. Bake until cheese has little bits of golden edges (typically 12-18 minutes)

### Notes
- **Generous yeast**: Extra yeast (2 tbsp) keeps the rise quick (originally tuned at 5,280 ft)
- **Maple syrup**: Feeds the yeast for faster activation and adds subtle sweetness
- **Semolina flour**: Provides excellent chew, crispy texture, and helps prevent sticking
- **Slightly sticky dough**: Don't over-flour - slightly sticky dough = better texture
- **Gluten development**: Work the dough enough to feel it become elastic and smooth
- **Baking paper trick**: Makes transfer foolproof, prevents sticking, can slide right onto stone
- **Temperature drop**: Starting at 290°C / 270°C fan / gas 9 [550°F] preheats the stone thoroughly, then 180°C / 160°C fan / gas 4 prevents burning <!-- CHECK -->
- **Visual doneness cue**: Golden edges on cheese = perfectly baked
- **Timing varies**: Rise time depends on room temperature - check for doubled size, not clock
- **Flour ratio**: ~2:1 semolina to plain flour by volume (~5:1 by weight), but adjust plain flour by feel for right texture
