# Pumpkin Parker House Rolls (Small Batch)

Autumn twist on classic Parker House with pumpkin purée - tender, golden-orange rolls perfect for Thanksgiving.

**Prep Time:** 15 min | **Rise Time:** 2-2.5 hrs | **Bake Time:** 20-25 min | **Yield:** 4 rolls

*Source: Adapted from Parker House Rolls + Sweet Potato Pull-Apart Rolls technique*

## Ingredients

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Plain flour | 90g + 1-2 tbsp for adjusting |
| Instant yeast | ¾ tsp (2g) |
| Caster sugar | 2¼ tsp (10g) |
| Salt | ⅓ tsp (2g) |
| Bicarbonate of soda | ⅛ tsp (pinch) |
| Pumpkin purée | 3-4 tbsp (~50g) |
| Unsalted butter (room temp) | 11g |
| Unsalted butter (melted, for brushing) | 14g |
| Buttermilk (room temp) | 3 tbsp (45ml) |
| Large egg | 1 tbsp beaten (~¼ egg) |

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix Pumpkin Base (5 minutes)
1. Mash pumpkin purée until smooth (no lumps)
2. Add room-temperature butter and buttermilk
3. Mix until well combined

### Step 2: Make Dough (5 minutes)
4. Combine flour, yeast, sugar, salt, and bicarbonate of soda in separate bowl
5. Add pumpkin mixture and beaten egg
6. Stir until shaggy dough forms

### Step 3: Knead (10 minutes by hand)
7. Turn dough onto lightly floured surface
8. Knead until smooth and elastic
9. **Altitude check**: If too wet, add 1-2 tbsp more flour

### Step 4: First Rise (60-75 minutes at altitude)
10. Place dough in greased bowl and cover
11. Let rise in warm spot until puffy

### Step 5: Shape Rolls (10 minutes)
12. Roll dough into 10 × 15cm rectangle on clean surface
13. Brush with melted butter
14. Cut lengthwise into two strips
15. Fold each strip so bottom edge extends 1cm beyond top
16. Cut into 4 rolls total

### Step 6: Second Rise (30-45 minutes)
17. Arrange rolls in greased 15cm tin
18. Brush with butter and cover
19. Let rise until puffy and touching
20. **Don't over-proof!** At altitude, dough rises faster and can collapse if left too long

### Step 7: Bake (20-25 minutes at 185°C / 165°C fan / gas 4)
21. Preheat oven to 185°C / 165°C fan / gas 4 about 15 minutes before rolls finish rising (altitude adjusted)
22. Bake until golden brown
23. Brush with butter immediately and cool 5 minutes

## Notes

- **Pumpkin purée**: Use tinned pure pumpkin (NOT pumpkin pie filling) or fresh roasted
- **Moisture adjustment**: Pumpkin adds liquid - may need extra flour
- **Flavor profile**: Subtle, earthy - pairs well with savory and sweet
- **Beautiful color**: Golden-orange hue even before baking
- **Holiday appeal**: Perfect for Thanksgiving
- **Storage**: Room temp 2-3 days, freeze up to 2 months

## Altitude Adjustments (5,280 ft)

- **Faster rise times**: Dough rises 25-35% faster at altitude - watch visual cues, not the clock
- **First rise**: 60-75 minutes (vs ~90 min at sea level) - look for puffy, airy dough
- **Second rise**: 30-45 minutes (vs 45-60 min at sea level) - rolls should be touching and puffy
- **Higher oven temp**: 185°C / 165°C fan / gas 4 (vs 180°C / 160°C fan / gas 4 at sea level) to set structure before over-expansion
- **Liquid vs. flour**: Flour is drier at altitude, but pumpkin adds moisture - you may need *extra* flour instead of extra liquid
- **Weigh the flour**: Use the 90g measurement for accuracy
- **Don't over-proof**: Most critical at altitude - over-proofed dough can collapse in the oven

## Serving Suggestions

- Honey butter or maple butter
- Cranberry sauce for holidays
- Alongside soups, stews, or roasted turkey
