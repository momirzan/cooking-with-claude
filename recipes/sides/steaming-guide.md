# Steaming Vegetables

Reference guide for steaming vegetables at sea level (converted from a 5,000+ ft guide: altitude times ran 2 minutes longer). <!-- CHECK -->

## Broccoli Steaming Times

| Time | Result |
|------|--------|
| **5 minutes** [7 at 5,000+ ft] | Very crisp, bright green, raw crunch still present |
| **6 minutes** [8 at 5,000+ ft] | Crisp-tender, ideal for most dishes ⭐ *recommended* |
| **7 minutes** [9 at 5,000+ ft] | Tender but not mushy, good for those who prefer softer |

*Broccoli times are 2 minutes less than the tested 5,000+ ft times.* <!-- CHECK -->

## Testing for Doneness

- Insert fork or knife tip into thickest part of stem
- Should pierce easily but still have slight resistance
- Broccoli should be bright green, not olive/drab

## Pro Tips for Steaming

1. **Cut uniformly**: 2.5-4cm pieces for even cooking
2. **Start checking at 5 minutes** to avoid overcooking [7 at 5,000+ ft] <!-- CHECK -->
3. **Thick stems**: Slice lengthwise so they cook as fast as florets
4. **Use tight-fitting lid** to maintain steam pressure
