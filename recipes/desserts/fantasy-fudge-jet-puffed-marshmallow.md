# Fantasy Fudge (Jet Puffed Marshmallow)
- **Prep Time**: 10 minutes
- **Cook Time**: 10-12 minutes
- **Chill Time**: 2-4 hours
- **Total Time**: 2.5-4.5 hours
- **Yield**: ~70 pieces (fills 23 × 33cm tin)
- **Difficulty**: Medium (sugar thermometer recommended)
- **Type**: Christmas Dessert / Classic American
- **Source**: Jet Puffed / Kraft Marshmallow Creme jar

### Ingredients
- [ ] **Caster sugar** (600g) - *Location: upstairs or downstairs pantry*
- [ ] **Salted butter** (170g, room temp, cubed) - *Location: upstairs fridge*
- [ ] **Evaporated milk** (150ml, from a 170g tin [1 can, 5 oz], room temp) - *Location: downstairs pantry* <!-- CHECK -->
- [ ] **Dark (semi-sweet) chocolate chips** (340g, room temp) - *Location: downstairs or upstairs pantry*
- [ ] **Marshmallow creme / Marshmallow Fluff** (200g [1 jar, 7 oz, Jet Puffed]) - *Location: pantry* <!-- CHECK -->
- [ ] **Vanilla extract** (1 tsp) - *Location: upstairs pantry*
- [ ] **Chopped walnuts** (120g, optional) - *Location: downstairs pantry*

### Equipment
- [ ] **23 × 33cm baking tin**
- [ ] **Baking paper**
- [ ] **Heavy-bottomed saucepan**
- [ ] **Sugar thermometer** (strongly recommended)
- [ ] **Spatula**
- [ ] **Timer**

### Instructions

#### 1. Prepare Tin
Line 23 × 33cm tin with baking paper, leaving overhang for easy removal

#### 2. Measure Everything First
Have all ingredients measured and near the hob - this moves fast once boiling starts!

#### 3. Cook the Base
1. Combine sugar, butter cubes, and evaporated milk in heavy-bottomed saucepan
2. Heat over medium, stirring constantly as butter melts and sugar dissolves
3. Bring to a rolling boil
4. **Cook to 112°C** (soft-ball stage) [107°C at 5,280 ft] <!-- CHECK -->
5. Or boil for approximately 5 minutes [4-4.5 min at 5,280 ft] <!-- CHECK -->
6. Remove from heat immediately when temperature is reached

#### 4. Add Remaining Ingredients
1. Add chocolate chips, stir until completely melted
2. Stir in marshmallow creme and vanilla, beat until combined
3. Fold in walnuts if using

#### 5. Pour and Chill
1. Pour into prepared tin
2. Smooth top with spatula
3. Cool completely at room temperature, then refrigerate until firm (2-4 hours)
4. Cut into 2.5cm squares

### Notes

**Sea-Level Conversion (tested 12/23/2025 at 5,280 ft, not yet at sea level):** <!-- CHECK -->
- **Pull fudge at 112°C** (sea-level soft-ball stage); the altitude version pulled at 107°C
- A sugar thermometer is the most reliable guide

**Critical Success Factors:**
- All ingredients at room temperature for even cooking
- Stir constantly to prevent scorching
- Don't rush - medium heat, not high
- Temperature is more reliable than timing

**Storage:**
- Room temperature: 2 weeks in airtight container
- Refrigerated: 1 month
- Frozen: Up to 4 months
- Best texture at room temp (fridge makes it harder)

**Serving:**
- Cut into cubes, stack on plate
- Can be stored and served at room temperature
- Classic Christmas candy

**Christmas Timeline:**
- Tested at altitude (5,280 ft) with great results; sea-level version not yet tested <!-- CHECK -->
