# All-Day Vegan French Onion Soup (High Altitude)

Cannellini • Soy • Dijon • White Miso  
Black Pepper • White Pepper • Light Sumac  
Low saturated fat • Bean-silk body • Deep caramelization  
Altitude tested at 5,280 ft

---

## Serves
4–6

---

## Ingredients

### Onion Base
- 1.4–1.8kg yellow onions, thinly sliced pole-to-pole
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ¾ tsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 180ml dry white wine (split use)

### Stock + Body
- 1.4–1.9 litres strong homemade vegetable stock
- 2 × 400g tins cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 2–3 tsp soy sauce (adjust to taste)
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1½–2 tsp white miso
- 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
- ½–1 tsp aged balsamic vinegar (to taste)

### Spice Structure
- ½ tsp sumac (used lightly, mostly for garnish)
- ⅛ tsp white pepper
- Generous freshly cracked black pepper

### Toast
- Baguette slices
- Nutritional yeast (for topping)
- Optional tiny dusting sumac per bowl

---

## Method

### 1. Slow Onion Collapse (2½–3 hours)

In a heavy Dutch oven (lidded casserole) over medium-low heat:

1. Add olive oil.
2. Add onions + salt.
3. Cover for 20 minutes to sweat.
4. Uncover and cook low for 2½–3 hours, stirring every 8–15 minutes.

At altitude:
- Keep heat slightly lower than instinct.
- Add small splashes of water if sticking aggressively.

Cook until onions are **deep chestnut / mahogany**, jam-like, and reduced ~75%.

---

### 2. Concentration Cycles

Increase heat slightly.

- Let fond develop.
- Deglaze with a splash of white wine + a few drops soy.
- Reduce fully.

Repeat 2–3 times.

Add tomato purée during the final cycle and cook until slightly darkened.

(Optional: stir in a small pinch of sumac and briefly bloom before final deglaze.)

---

### 3. Stock + Bean Integration (45–60 minutes)

Add:
- Vegetable stock
- Cannellini beans

Bring to a gentle simmer.
Cook uncovered or partially covered depending on desired reduction.

After ~30 minutes:

- Remove 720–960ml (beans + broth + onions).
- Blend completely smooth.
- Return to pot.

Simmer another 10–15 minutes until broth lightly coats a spoon.

---

### 4. Final Architecture (Gentle Heat Only)

Lower heat.

Add in order:

1. Dijon (whisked into hot broth first)
2. White pepper
3. White miso (whisked into hot broth first)
4. Nutritional yeast (whisked into hot broth first)
5. Soy sauce (adjust gradually)

Simmer gently 5–7 minutes.

Add balsamic in ¼ tsp increments until the soup feels rounded but not acidic.

Finish with generous freshly cracked black pepper.

Rest 3–5 minutes before serving.

---

## Toast

Toast baguette at 180°C / 160°C fan / gas 4 until crisp.

While warm:
- Sprinkle nutritional yeast.
- Optional light brush olive oil.

Float on soup.

Garnish individual bowls with:
- Tiny dusting of sumac
- Fresh cracked black pepper

---

## Texture Target

Broth should:
- Lightly coat a spoon
- Feel velvety from blended beans
- Not read as stew
- Not read as thin broth

---

## Flavor Notes

Deep caramelized sweetness  
Savory fermented backbone  
Rounded mid-palate  
Warm pepper structure  
Bright lift from light sumac  

Elegant, structured, plant-forward French onion.
