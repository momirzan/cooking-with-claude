# Cold Tomato-Strawberry Gazpacho

**Source:** Original recipe (strawberry rescue!) · **Makes:** ~1.4 litres (4 starters / 2 light meals) · **Difficulty:** Easy · **No-cook** · ~15 min + chill
A bright, strawberry-forward chilled gazpacho. Blend, balance, chill. Great use for berries about to turn.

## Ingredients
- **450g strawberries**, hulled (full 450g — strawberry-forward version)
- **~900g tomatoes**, cored & chopped — heirloom + cherry tomatoes *(bump tomato up to balance the full 450g of berries)*
- **1 medium cucumber**, peeled & chopped
- **½–1 pepper** (red/orange), chopped — savory body
- **1 small garlic clove** *(start small — raw garlic grows in the blend)*
- optional **¼ shallot**
- **2–3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil** (a medium-fruity oil)
- **1½–2 tbsp vinegar** — apple cider vinegar (keeps color bright) *or* a splash of aged balsamic for depth; *more* than a plain gazpacho to offset the berry sweetness
- **Squeeze of lime** + **salt, generously**
- pinch **smoked paprika** + cracked black pepper

**If short on fresh tomatoes:**
- Best stretcher: thaw ~120–240ml **frozen homemade tomato sauce** — real tomato; it's cooked + seasoned, so **go easy on salt**.
- Booster only: **1–2 tbsp tomato purée** to deepen color/umami — not a bulk substitute (concentrated, cooked-tasting).

## Method
1. Blend everything until smooth (or leave a little texture). **Strain** through a coarse sieve for silky texture + to remove strawberry seeds.
2. **Taste and correct aggressively toward savory:** salt → acid (vinegar/lime) → more tomato, until it reads like a bright fruity *soup*, not a smoothie. The pepper, garlic, olive oil, and salt pull it back from sweet.
3. **Chill hard, 1–2 hrs minimum** — gazpacho must be cold, and flavors meld as it rests. Make ahead.

## Serve
Cold, garnished with diced cucumber + strawberry, a drizzle of lemon olive oil (or good EVOO + a little lemon zest), cracked pepper, torn herbs (parsley; no basil needed).

## Notes
- ⭐ **Food rescue:** a great home for strawberries near their date + surplus tomatoes. Raw and plant-forward (tomato = lycopene, berries = antioxidants).
- **Balance is everything** — with a full 450g of berries, push salt + acid harder than instinct says.
- Keeps 2–3 days refrigerated; stir before serving.
