# From-Scratch Udon with Mushroom Dashi

- **Prep Time**: 45 minutes (includes dough resting)
- **Cook Time**: 25 minutes
- **Total Time**: 1 hour 10 minutes
- **Servings**: 4
- **Difficulty**: Medium-Hard
- **Type**: Dinner, Weekend Project
- **Tags**: vegan, noodles, from-scratch, Japanese-inspired, weekend-project
- **Source**: Original recipe

## Nutritional Estimate (Per Serving)
- **Protein**: 14g
- **Fiber**: 4g
- **Fat**: 6g
- **Calories**: 380

## Ingredients

### Handmade Udon Noodles
- [ ] **Plain flour** (300g)
- [ ] **Salt** (1½ tsp)
- [ ] **Warm water** (150ml)
- [ ] **Cornflour or potato starch** (for dusting)

### Mushroom Dashi Stock
- [ ] **Dried shiitake mushrooms** (6-8, about 30g)
- [ ] **Kombu** (1 large piece, about 10 × 15cm)
- [ ] **Water** (1.4 litres)
- [ ] **Soy sauce** (3 tbsp)
- [ ] **Mirin** (2 tbsp)
- [ ] **Rice vinegar** (1 tsp)

### Toppings
- [ ] **Fresh shiitake or mixed mushrooms** (225g, sliced)
- [ ] **Firm tofu** (170g, sliced into rectangles)
- [ ] **Spring onions** (4, sliced)
- [ ] **Sesame oil** (1 tsp)
- [ ] **Spinach or bok choy** (60g [2 cups]) <!-- CHECK -->
- [ ] **Shichimi togarashi** (seven-spice, optional)
- [ ] **Nori strips**

## Instructions

### Phase 1: Start Dashi (5 minutes active + soak time)
1. Combine dried shiitakes and kombu in 1.4 litres cold water
2. Let soak at least 30 minutes (or overnight in fridge for deeper flavor)
3. When ready, heat slowly over medium-low — remove kombu just before water boils
4. Simmer with shiitakes 10 minutes, then strain
5. Add soy sauce, mirin, and rice vinegar to the stock

### Phase 2: Make Udon Dough (15 minutes + 30 minutes rest)
1. Dissolve salt in warm water
2. Add flour to a large bowl, pour in salted water
3. Mix with chopsticks until shaggy, then knead by hand 5 minutes
4. The dough will be stiff — this is correct
5. Form into a ball, wrap in cling film, and rest 30 minutes at room temperature
6. **Kneading trick**: Place dough in a zip-lock bag, step on it with your feet to knead flat, fold, and repeat 3 times

### Phase 3: Roll and Cut Noodles (15 minutes)
1. Dust work surface generously with starch
2. Roll dough to about 3mm thick
3. Fold dough in thirds (like a letter)
4. Cut into 5mm strips with a sharp knife
5. Shake noodles loose, dust with more starch

### Phase 4: Cook and Assemble (10 minutes)
1. Bring a large pot of unsalted water to a rolling boil
2. Cook udon 8-10 minutes, stirring gently — they should be chewy, not soft
3. While noodles cook, sauté fresh mushrooms and tofu in sesame oil until golden
4. Wilt spinach/bok choy in the hot dashi

### To Serve
Divide noodles among bowls, ladle hot dashi over them, arrange mushrooms, tofu, and greens on top. Finish with spring onions, nori strips, and shichimi togarashi.

## Notes
- The dough is intentionally stiff — resist adding more water. Resting relaxes the gluten
- Stepping on the dough in a bag is the traditional Japanese method and actually works best
- Fresh udon has a completely different texture from dried — bouncy and chewy
- Extra uncooked noodles freeze well: dust with starch, portion on a baking tray, freeze flat, then bag
- If short on time, use store-bought fresh or frozen udon and just make the dashi
