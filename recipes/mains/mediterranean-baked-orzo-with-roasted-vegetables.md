# Mediterranean Baked Orzo with Roasted Vegetables

- **Prep Time**: 20 minutes
- **Cook Time**: 40 minutes
- **Total Time**: 60 minutes
- **Servings**: 6
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Type**: Dinner
- **Tags**: vegan-adaptable, Mediterranean, pasta, meal-prep, one-pot
- **Source**: Original recipe

## Nutritional Estimate (Per Serving)
- **Protein**: 11g
- **Fiber**: 5g
- **Fat**: 12g
- **Calories**: 360

## Ingredients

- [ ] **Orzo pasta** (450g)
- [ ] **Cherry tomatoes** (300g [1 pint], halved) <!-- CHECK -->
- [ ] **Courgette** (1 large, diced)
- [ ] **Red pepper** (1, diced)
- [ ] **Red onion** (1 medium, diced)
- [ ] **Kalamata olives** (65g [½ cup], pitted and halved) <!-- CHECK -->
- [ ] **Artichoke hearts** (1 × 400g tin, drained and quartered)
- [ ] **Garlic** (4 cloves, minced)
- [ ] **Vegetable stock** (600ml)
- [ ] **Olive oil** (3 tbsp)
- [ ] **Dried oregano** (1½ tsp)
- [ ] **Smoked paprika** (½ tsp)
- [ ] **Crushed red pepper flakes** (¼ tsp)
- [ ] **Lemon zest and juice** (1 lemon)
- [ ] **Salt and pepper** (to taste)

### Optional Toppings
- [ ] **Vegan feta or regular feta** (75g crumbled)
- [ ] **Fresh basil** (handful, torn)
- [ ] **Pine nuts** (35g, toasted)
- [ ] **Nutritional yeast** (for vegan cheesy flavor)

## Instructions

### Phase 1: Roast Vegetables (20 minutes)
1. Preheat oven to 200°C / 180°C fan / gas 6
2. Toss cherry tomatoes, courgette, red pepper, and red onion with 2 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking tray
3. Roast 20 minutes until caramelized

### Phase 2: Cook Orzo (while vegetables roast)
1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large ovenproof frying pan or Dutch oven (lidded casserole) over medium heat
2. Add garlic, cook 30 seconds
3. Add orzo, stir to coat in oil, toast 1 minute
4. Pour in vegetable stock, oregano, smoked paprika, and red pepper flakes
5. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer 8-9 minutes until orzo is al dente and stock is mostly absorbed

### Phase 3: Combine & Bake (15 minutes)
1. Fold roasted vegetables, olives, and artichoke hearts into the orzo
2. Top with crumbled feta or nutritional yeast if using
3. Bake uncovered at 200°C / 180°C fan / gas 6 for 12-15 minutes until top is lightly golden
4. Remove from oven, stir in lemon zest and juice

### To Serve
Top with fresh basil and toasted pine nuts. Serve warm or at room temperature.

## Notes
- The orzo absorbs liquid as it sits — add a splash of stock when reheating
- Keeps well in fridge for 4 days; great for meal prep lunches
- Swap vegetables seasonally — butternut squash and kale work great in winter
- Add chickpeas for extra protein
