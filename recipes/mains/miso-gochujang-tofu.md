# Miso-Gochujang Tofu Traybake

Easy weeknight vegan meal with crispy tofu and roasted vegetables.

**Prep Time:** 10 min (+ 30 min marinating) | **Cook Time:** 25-30 min | **Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

### Marinade
| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed | 1 block (400-450g) |
| Miso paste | 2 tbsp |
| Gochujang | 1 tbsp (adjust to taste) |
| Soy sauce | 2 tbsp |
| Sesame oil | 1 tbsp |
| Fish sauce (optional) | 1 tsp |
| Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| Fresh ginger, grated | 2.5cm |
| Rice vinegar (optional) | 1 tsp |

### Vegetables
| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Green beans, trimmed | 450g |
| Onions, cut into wedges | 2 medium |
| Oil for roasting | 2 tbsp |
| Salt and pepper | to taste |

## Instructions

1. **Prep tofu**: Press to remove water, cut into 2.5cm cubes
2. **Make marinade**: Whisk miso, gochujang, soy sauce, sesame oil, fish sauce, garlic, and ginger
3. **Marinate**: Coat tofu and let sit 30+ minutes (longer is better)
4. **Prep vegetables**: Preheat oven to 200°C / 180°C fan / gas 6. Toss beans and onions with oil, salt, pepper
5. **Arrange**: Place tofu and vegetables on baking tray with space between pieces
6. **Roast**: Bake 25-30 minutes until tofu is golden and veggies have charred edges

## Notes

- **Tofu texture**: Press well for crispier results (15+ minutes under weight)
- **Don't overcrowd**: Space on pan = proper browning
- **Marinate ahead**: Up to 24 hours for deeper flavor
- **Storage**: Leftovers keep 3-4 days refrigerated
- **Reheat**: 200°C / 180°C fan / gas 6 oven 5-10 minutes to restore crispiness

## Spice Levels

- **Mild**: 1 tsp gochujang
- **Medium**: 1 tbsp gochujang (as written)
- **Spicy**: 2+ tbsp gochujang

## Variations

**Flavor:**
- **Korean-style**: Add kimchi to the baking tray in the last 10 minutes
- **Thai-style**: Replace miso with 2 tbsp peanut butter, add 1 tsp curry powder
- **Mediterranean**: Use tahini instead of miso, add olives and cherry tomatoes

**Protein swaps:**
- **Tempeh**: Cut into strips, marinate the same way
- **Cauliflower**: Cut into florets, increase cook time to 35 minutes
- **Chicken thighs**: Boneless thighs work great with this marinade

**Vegetable swaps:**
- **Broccoli and peppers**: Cut to similar sizes
- **Brussels sprouts and carrots**: Halve sprouts, slice carrots on the diagonal
- **Courgette and cherry tomatoes**: Add tomatoes in the last 15 minutes

## Tasting Notes

**What worked in testing:**
- Miso and gochujang balanced well; green beans and onions roasted nicely alongside
- Finishing with fresh lime, a splash of soy sauce, coriander, and garlic chili sauce lifted the whole dish

**For crispier tofu next time:**
- Cut into uniform 2.5cm cubes rather than crumbling — irregular pieces don't crisp evenly
- Press longer (45+ minutes)
- Dust with cornflour before marinating
- Try 220°C / 200°C fan / gas 7, or grill the last 2-3 minutes

## Serving Suggestions

- Over steamed rice or quinoa
- With kimchi or pickled vegetables
- Topped with sesame seeds and spring onions
- Fresh lime squeeze, extra soy sauce, coriander, garlic chili sauce
