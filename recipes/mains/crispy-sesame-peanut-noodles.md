# Crispy Sesame-Peanut Noodles with Roasted Broccoli

- **Prep Time**: 15 minutes
- **Cook Time**: 25 minutes
- **Total Time**: 40 minutes
- **Servings**: 4
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Type**: Dinner
- **Tags**: vegan, noodles, Asian-inspired, high-protein, meal-prep
- **Source**: Original recipe

## Nutritional Estimate (Per Serving)
- **Protein**: 16g
- **Fiber**: 6g
- **Fat**: 18g
- **Calories**: 420

## Ingredients

### Peanut Sauce
- [ ] **Natural peanut butter** (65g)
- [ ] **Soy sauce or tamari** (3 tbsp)
- [ ] **Rice vinegar** (2 tbsp)
- [ ] **Sesame oil** (1 tbsp)
- [ ] **Maple syrup** (1 tbsp)
- [ ] **Sriracha** (1-2 tsp)
- [ ] **Garlic** (2 cloves, grated)
- [ ] **Fresh ginger** (2.5cm piece, grated)
- [ ] **Lime juice** (1 tbsp)
- [ ] **Hot water** (2-3 tbsp, to thin)

### Noodles & Vegetables
- [ ] **Lo mein noodles or spaghetti** (340g)
- [ ] **Broccoli crowns** (450g, cut into florets)
- [ ] **Edamame, shelled** (150g [1 cup]) <!-- CHECK -->
- [ ] **Red cabbage** (70g, shredded [1 cup]) <!-- CHECK -->
- [ ] **Neutral oil** (1 tbsp, for roasting)

### Garnish
- [ ] **Crushed roasted peanuts** (30g)
- [ ] **Sesame seeds**
- [ ] **Spring onions** (3, sliced)
- [ ] **Fresh coriander**
- [ ] **Lime wedges**

## Instructions

### Phase 1: Roast Broccoli (25 minutes)
1. Preheat oven to 220°C / 200°C fan / gas 7
2. Toss broccoli florets with neutral oil, salt, and pepper
3. Spread on baking tray in single layer
4. Roast 20-25 minutes until edges are crispy and charred

### Phase 2: Make Sauce & Cook Noodles (while broccoli roasts)
1. Whisk all sauce ingredients together until smooth; set aside
2. Cook noodles according to package directions
3. Drain and rinse with cold water to stop cooking
4. Toss noodles with a drizzle of sesame oil to prevent sticking

### Phase 3: Assemble
1. Toss noodles with peanut sauce until well coated
2. Add edamame and shredded red cabbage, toss
3. Top with roasted broccoli
4. Garnish with crushed peanuts, sesame seeds, spring onions, coriander, and lime wedges

## Notes
- Sauce can be made 3 days ahead — it thickens in the fridge, thin with water
- Great cold or at room temperature for lunch the next day
- Swap broccoli for roasted cauliflower, sweet potato, or Brussels sprouts
- For extra protein, add baked tofu or tempeh
